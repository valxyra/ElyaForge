Welcome to Elya Forge

For warriors whose hands tremble with blunt swords after clashing with Draugr, Elya offers blades sharpened as sharp as the sun at the top of Throat of the World. For adventurers afflicted with Bone Break Fever, she prepares a cure potion that works faster than Close Wounds spells. And for wizards whose souls thirst for power, Elya can turn a simple wooden staff into a meteor shower summoning weapon. In Elya's hands, each order is not just an inanimate object, but an extension of the wearer's soul — Elya Forge is here to answer your needs.

From the flames of the forge to your grasp — Let's create legends together.

Visit Elya's workshop at Lakeview Manor, Falkreath Hold.

Elya Forge Services

Elya the Bosmer, with nimble hands inherited from her Valenwood ancestors and knowledge honed in the heart of Skyrim, provides the finest crafting services in all of Tamriel. From pounding metal forges to magically rustling potions, each piece is crafted with the precision and dedication of Y'ffre's sacred art.

Forging

Swords that slice through the wind, armor that challenges the dragon's claws, or jewelry that would make nobles envious — Elya's forging combines Bosmer aesthetics with Nord toughness.

Alchemy

A potion that heals war wounds, a poison that paralyzes a Falmer, or a magic-boosting elixir? Elya's laboratory is full of sparkling ingredients from common mountain flowers to the valuable Daedra Heart.

Enchanting

Want your sword to blaze like the fury of Mehrunes Dagon? Or armor that protects from the shouts of Fus Ro Dah? Elya imbues the gear with magic effects to give it unimaginable power.
